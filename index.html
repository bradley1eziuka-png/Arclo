FAQ

Common questions.

How long does a project actually take?

A custom mockup is typically ready within 2 business days of our first call. Full build and launch timing depends on scope. Most single-page sites move fast; multi-page builds take longer. You'll get a clear timeline before any work starts.

Can I edit or update the site myself after it launches?

Your site is custom-coded, not built on a drag-and-drop platform like Wix or WordPress. That's part of what makes it fast and clean. If something needs to change after launch, that's covered under ongoing maintenance, or you can just reach out directly for a quick update. You're never stuck waiting on a support ticket.

What happens after the site launches, do you disappear?

No. Every site includes ongoing maintenance, so it keeps working after launch. Reach out directly any time something needs to change, no ticket number, no hand-off, just a reply from the person who built it.

Do I need to provide my own copy, photos, and content?

We'll work with whatever you have: existing copy, photos, logos, and fill gaps together during the consult. You don't need a polished brief walking in.
