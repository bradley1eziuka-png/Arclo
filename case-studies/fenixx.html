The Client

A precision manufacturer serving the most demanding buyers in the country.

Fenixx Technologies is a waterjet cutting manufacturer based in Fraser, MI, ITAR registered, serving aerospace and defense procurement engineers.

The Problem

The site looked outdated, and buyers had no way to request a quote online.

The technical information was all there: materials, machine specs, tolerances, laid out plainly. But the design looked dated next to a modern manufacturing operation, and there was no structured way to request a quote. Just a generic contact page, with no path built around how their sales team actually qualifies a request.

The Strategy

Built around what procurement engineers actually evaluate.

Built the site around what procurement and engineering buyers actually evaluate: machine capabilities, materials, facility scale, and compliance credentials like ITAR registration. The quote form was designed around Fenixx's real sales workflow, the exact fields their team needed to qualify a request. Buyers with design files to share can send them directly by email once the conversation starts.

The Quote Form

Built around how Fenixx's team actually qualifies a request.

Instead of a generic contact form, the quote request was built around the exact information Fenixx needs to qualify a manufacturing job.
