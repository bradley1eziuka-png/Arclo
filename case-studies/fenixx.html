Case Study

Fenixx Technologies

Waterjet cutting manufacturer, Fraser, MI. Aerospace & defense procurement.

BH

Bill Hayter, Owner

The Client

A precision manufacturer serving the most demanding buyers in the country.

Fenixx Technologies is a waterjet cutting manufacturer based in Fraser, MI, ITAR registered, serving aerospace and defense procurement engineers.

The Problem

Technical buyers had no way to evaluate real capability.

Their site gave technical buyers no way to evaluate real capability: no tolerances, no machine specs, no materials list, and no clear path to actually request a quote online. Serious prospects had no reason to trust the site and no easy way to act on it.

The Strategy

Built around what procurement engineers actually evaluate.

Built the site around what procurement and engineering buyers actually evaluate: tolerances, machine capabilities, materials, facility details. The quote form was designed around Fenixx's real sales workflow, the exact fields their team needed to qualify a request, including file upload support for design files.

The Results

Real traction, fast.
